## Post

This is what happens when you stop testing AI CAD on sensible objects.

Built inside Autodesk Fusion through MCP, the assembly stacks concentric rings, repeated segments, intersecting shafts, outer loops, and a dense central mechanism into one project.

The interesting part is that it still holds together when you start rotating and inspecting it. The repeated geometry stays aligned, the structures share the same center, and the model does not immediately collapse into decorative nonsense.

That is a much better benchmark than another gear or bracket. The harder test is how weird the assembly can get before the agent loses the plot.
