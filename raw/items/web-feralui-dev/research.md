# Research

## What it is

FeralUI (feralui.dev) v0.1.1: experimental component library whose parts “swing, grab, crumple, and catch the light” — physically inspired motion rather than standard fade/slide kits.

## How it works

- Homepage catalog: PullCord, Claw, Captcha, Gradient Builder, MotionView, Screenery, DeskFolio, Blinds, Blob, AniMaps, Fur, Vacuum, Crumple, Hologram, Matchday.
- Drop-in components with unusual affordances (cords, crumple, hologram) — closer to art-direction toys than shadcn primitives.
- Early (0.1.1); expect instability vs Cult UI / Vengeance.
- Useful as taste references for agents that otherwise emit rectangular cards.

## Why saved

Anti-slop physicality for heroes and playful marketing. Complements Opale taste essay and Three.js graphics skills when the brief wants tactile weirdness.
