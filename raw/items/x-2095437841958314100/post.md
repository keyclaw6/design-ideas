This is insane: Hyper3D just dropped WorldGen, an AI that turns ONE photo into a full interactive 3D world 

Yingmu Technology's new model is built on CAST, the tech that won a Best Paper Award at SIGGRAPH 2025, a prize only Google, Meta, and Hyper3D took home that year.

Here's what makes it wild:

> Feed it a single 2D image, and it picks out every object, fills in the hidden parts, and rebuilds each one as its own separate 3D model
> Tables, chairs, small props: all independently movable, swappable, and editable
> Physics comes built in. It figures out collisions, mass, friction, and how objects actually sit or lean on each other
> You can also manually select just one object to generate

And it's not just a demo toy. Assets drop straight into Blender, Unity, and Unreal Engine, ready for robot training simulations, film production, game dev, and XR.

The really wild part: pair it with video models like Seedance 2.5, and you get a full pipeline. WorldGen builds the 3D scene and spatial structure first, then the video model adds character performance, materials, lighting, and style on top, turning basic renders into near-cinematic footage.

The line between 3D generation, game engines, and AI video is basically gone at this point.
