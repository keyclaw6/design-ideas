## What it is

Pipeline example: Higgsfield Seedance 2.5 for animation, Cursor Composer 2.5 for implementation, using github.com/elayadesign/ai-design-skills.

## How it works

- Seedance is the video/motion generator (same family as other Seedance camera-control posts in the library).
- Composer 2.5 is the coding agent applying design skills to the site/UI.
- Skill pack: elayadesign/ai-design-skills — installable design/motion knowledge for Cursor.
- Shows a split: generative video for the moving hero, skills for the surrounding craft.
- Pairs with MiniMax H3/Runway unlimited (`x-2090098441200517416`) as alternative video backends.

## Why saved

Exactly the BESS landing shape: video/flythrough plate plus agent-built page chrome.
