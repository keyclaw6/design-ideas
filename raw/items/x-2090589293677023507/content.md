The core claim checks out. For years 3D Gaussian Splatting quality was solid, but huge files, missing streaming/LOD, and weak deployment blocked practical use of large scenes.  Advances now deliver LOD systems, progressive streaming, SPZ compression, collision meshes, and true browser rendering. ManycoreTech’s open-source Aholo Viewer demonstrates city-scale scenes with over a billion splats running directly in the browser. 3DGS has moved from research demo to usable infrastructure.
