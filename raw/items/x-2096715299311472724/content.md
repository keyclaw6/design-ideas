## Post

Best methods to get inbound leads on LinkedIn as a B2B founder ($0 to your first 10 closed deals):

$0:
- founder profile over the company page (nobody follows a logo)
- profile funnel (banner, headline, featured section that actually sends somewhere)
- voice database trained on how you talk, not your website copy
- 3-5 team accounts posting (5 accounts = 5 shots at the algo daily)
- opinion posts (opinions get comments, tips get likes)
- lead magnet posts ("comment GUIDE" → comment flood → warm DMs)
- 2-3 new lead magnets a month until you have a library to recycle
- sales call objections turned into posts (your prospects already told you what to write)
- client case studies with the actual numbers, not "great results"
- pull what's trending on X and bring it to LinkedIn a day early
- Dream 100 commenting (on posts your buyers are already reading)
- daily connection requests to ICP with no pitch attached
- DMs to everyone who engaged with your last 3 posts
- screen recorded demos and clips pulled from your own calls
- LinkedIn newsletter (subscribers get notified, followers don't)
- podcast guesting, then clip it and post it yourself
- cold email with an artifact that points back at your profile
