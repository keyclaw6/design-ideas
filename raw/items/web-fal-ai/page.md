## Three product pillars

| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Model APIs** | 1,000+ production-ready image/video/audio/3D models via unified API |
| **Serverless** | On-demand GPUs; fal Inference Engine; zero-to-thousands scaling |
| **Compute** | Dedicated H100/H200/B200 clusters for training and fine-tuning |
