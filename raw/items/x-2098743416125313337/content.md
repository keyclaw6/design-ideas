Screenwriting Skills Now Cover Television
GitHub: https://github.com/jtydhr88/screenwriting-skills

This screenwriting skill pack used to serve feature films only. It now covers television: 13 skills → 20, with 7 new series-layer skills.

The series layer works by replacement: when you write a series, "engine plus season" takes over from "a three-act structure designed for one film."

Sources are 15 television craft books plus the published scripts themselves. The whole pack now stands at 32 books and 12 volumes of published scripts, covering film, television and the stage.
