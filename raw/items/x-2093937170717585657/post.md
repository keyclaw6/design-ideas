How about one image straight to an editable UE5 or Blender scene?

Lumera just dropped paper - Engine-Native Editable 3D World Reconstruction with Objects & Lighting.

You give it an image. It figures out what's in the scene, rebuilds every object as a separate mesh, then recreates the lighting as actual engine lights you can move and tweak. Even pulls an HDR environment out of the shot.

The lighting part is the big deal here. Every other image-to-3D approach bakes it in. Lumera keeps it a parameter, like the scene was built in the engine from the start.
