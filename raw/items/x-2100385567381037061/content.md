Astra is so insanely great at making motion design animations for your product. Here's how we do it:

1. give it access to your codebase and encourage it to reference actual components
2. dictate what you want the progression to be. ask for your animation to be an "abstraction", not a product literal
3. ask Astra to create image mockup stills of what the animation will be
4. give feedback, then let it cook on the animation (it will use the stills as references)

Feels like everyone on our DX team is following a pattern like this these days
