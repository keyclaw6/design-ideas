## What it is

Surprise-find tweet for 23rd.dev: a shadcn/ui registry of opinionated React/Svelte components (shaders, backgrounds, footers, interactive UI), CLI-installable, source owned by the consumer.

## How it works

- Positioning: 'for people who ship' — less community dump than 21st.dev, more curated.
- Keywords on-site: Tailwind, WebGL/shaders, Motion, Next.js and Svelte.
- Author/publisher radiumcoders (Jay); GitHub radiumcoders/23rd.dev.
- Install via shadcn-style registry CLI; copy is owned locally after add.
- Useful as a motion/shader component source for marketing pages without a full Three.js app.
