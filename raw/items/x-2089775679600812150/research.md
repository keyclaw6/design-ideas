## What it is

Grind-log that actually ships three scroll-driven components on great-ui.com: word-focus-scroll, split-line-fly-in, blur-scroll-reveal.

## How it works

- word-focus-scroll — typographic emphasis tied to scroll position.
- split-line-fly-in — line-splitting entrance, common hero headline grammar.
- blur-scroll-reveal — blur-to-sharp on scroll (safer than heavy 3D for text sections).
- The LeetCode lines are noise; the three URLs are the keepable artifacts.
- Drop-in references for BESS page copy sections between 3D heroes.

## Why saved

Need named scroll-typography recipes, not another generic AOS fade.
