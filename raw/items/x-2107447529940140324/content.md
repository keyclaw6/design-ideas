## Post

"Local SEO iS EaSY"

Local SEO:

• Set hours past 9pm
• Reply to every review
• Photos of your own team
• 2 to 3 profile posts a week
• Every service listed separately
• One primary category, chosen on purpose
• Ask for the review the day the job closes
• Each town you cover added as a service area
• Get your details onto 80 to 100 directories
• Send the review link by text as well as email
• Match your name, address and phone everywhere
• Track your rank on a grid across the whole area
• Keep a call button on screen for mobile visitors
• Follow up 2 to 3 times, people say yes and forget
• Build a page for every service you want calls for
• Link each town page back to its main service page
• Booking calendar near the top of every service page
• Answer the phone after hours or lose the 9pm searches
• Put the service and city in the H1, title and description
• Give every nearby town its own page with local detail on it
• A case value calculator ranks on its own and collects the lead
• Write for people about to hire you, how-to guides pull in DIYers
• Links from sites in your trade or your city are the ones Google trusts
• Marketplace "DR 70" links get their traffic from countries you don't serve
• Fill in every field on Yelp, it's the source ChatGPT quotes most for local answers
• Search the #1 competitor's name in quotes and compare their citation count to yours
• Count how many reviews the top 3 got in the last 90 days, that's the number you're chasing
• Website changes take 2 to 4 weeks to count, so judge the profile in days and the site in months
• Going from 3rd to 2nd roughly doubles the calls, and 2nd to 1st doubles them again, so finish the search you're closest on first
• When you rebrand or move, hunt down every listing with the old name, number or address, because Google reads it as a second business
• Run your 20 most common customer questions through ChatGPT every month, and every one you're missing from becomes the next page you build
