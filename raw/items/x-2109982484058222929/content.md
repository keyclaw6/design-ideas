Before spending thousands learning marketing through trial and error, send these repos to your agent

5 GitHub repos for positioning, SEO, growth strategy, and the marketing frameworks behind successful businesses

1. Marketing and SEO Playbook

https://github.com/khasky/marketing-and-seo-playbook

Learn how SEO and marketing tactics fit into a broader strategy for attracting customers

2. OpenCMO

https://github.com/About-Intelligence/OpenCMO

Study real-world marketing playbooks covering launches, growth, pricing, and customer acquisition

3. Growth Marketing OS

https://github.com/growthack88/growth-marketing-os

Explore growth frameworks and experiments to understand how businesses acquire and retain customers

4. Marketing Lab

https://github.com/alphagammamle/Marketing-Lab

Learn core marketing concepts like segmentation, targeting, positioning, and pricing through practical exercises

5. Awesome Marketing

https://github.com/marketingtoolslist/awesome-marketing

Find marketing resources to deepen your knowledge across different channels and disciplines
