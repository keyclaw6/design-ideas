## Post

Holy crap, who the hell did this?!!

Someone open-sourced a UGC video studio and lets you run it on your own computer.

GitHub's already at 4,047 stars, repo's called OpenShorts.

You just paste your product link, and it writes the script, creates an AI actor, gives them a voice, and syncs their lips. B-roll, subtitles, hook overlays - it's got all that covered too.

And it's not just ads. Feed it a long video and it'll find the good bits, follow the speaker's face, and turn them into vertical clips for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Thumbnails, titles, descriptions - they threw those in too.

No camera, no filming yourself, no watermark on the self-hosted version

Link's right here: http://github.com/mutonby/openshorts

The catch? AI actors and voices still use paid APIs.
The studio runs on your machine; those models don't. 

But hey, the studio itself is free, and you can go from a product link to a talking spokesperson without picking up a camera. Who needs a damn film crew!
