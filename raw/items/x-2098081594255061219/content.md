Introducing http://Bencho.dev

A library of interactive UI blocks you can explore, tune, bench, and take into your own projects.

Started as a spare-time experiment to see what happens when my design thinking and style meet Claude, pushing further into interactions, components, and the details that make interfaces feel good.

Then it became something worth building properly.

New blocks every week. 
More to come. Excited for this 🐐🔥
