# Research

## What it is
Jason Zhou introducing loop-library: a catalog of agent loops that “actually worked” in production, each with a prompt you can paste into your agent. Open-sourced at https://loopany.ai/templates

## How it works
- Library of named loops (research, build, review, etc.) rather than one mega-agent.
- Each template is a prompt/workflow you copy into Cursor/Claude/Codex.
- Positioning: most public “loops” are theater; these are claimed field-tested.
- Screenshot/video on disk shows the template gallery UI.

## Why saved
KB is assembling agent workflows; this is a browseable index of loops to steal, next to the Aman primer and Anthropic cookbooks.
