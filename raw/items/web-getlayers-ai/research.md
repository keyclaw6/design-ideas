# Research

## What it is

GetLayers AI: commercial library of copy-paste prompts that recreate self-contained HTML pages — Sections (animated blocks), Backgrounds, Gradients (~1 KB WebGL), 3D Scenes, UI templates. Tagline: “Your AI builds generic — we make it cinematic.”

## How it works

- Tune 3D scenes and GLSL gradients in-browser, then copy the prompt for Claude/Cursor/v0/Bolt (stack-agnostic).
- Compose scene + gradient + UI into one prompt; MCP for assembling a whole site from the library.
- Next.js starter for production rebuilds; free tier with curated full prompts.
- Example scenes: Orb, Rift Stone, Pinwheel Galaxy; sections include carousel, roadmap, glass wave.
- Complements SceneAI (section prompts) and scroll-craft (grammars/QA).

## Why saved

Prompt-level cinematic layers for heroes without owning Three.js from scratch. MCP assembly is the productized version of MengTo capture→prompt.
