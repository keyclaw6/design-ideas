# Research

## What it is

TinyShelf (tinyshelf.co): curated, human-reviewed software directory — browse by category/tag. Contrasts padded directory lists. Submit via Google OAuth + editorial review.

## How it works

- Categories at capture: Productivity 106, Marketing 79, AI 62, Dev tools 48, Design 38, Analytics & SEO 37, Photo/Video 30; 19 categories, paginated homepage.
- TinyShots listed under tools; ecosystem with TinyLaunch DR service.
- Positioning: quality over volume — useful both as a backlink target and as a discovery surface “for the AI search era” (copy on some listings).
- Not a bulk submit API; one reviewed listing.

## Why saved

High-signal directory for product launches (BESS-adjacent software, design tools). Pair with TinyLaunch for breadth vs TinyShelf for curation.
