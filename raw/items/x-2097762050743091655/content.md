# 31,000 cold emails a month without spam: 2 mailboxes per domain, 15/day cap, 50 domains

## Post

You can send 31,000 cold emails a month and still stay out of spam.

The teams landing in spam scaled volume without keeping the mailbox ratio intact.

The math we run:

→ 2 mailboxes per 1 domain
→ 15 emails per day per mailbox (never more)
→ 50 domains and 100 mailboxes get you to 31,000 emails a month

Run that through a two-step sequence and it turns into roughly 15,000 prospects a month.

Over a full quarter that's tens of thousands of prospects reached, all without torching deliverability.

Want more volume? Leave the daily cap alone and just add domains and mailboxes at the same 2-to-1 ratio.

Remake low-performing inboxes consistently and that’s the basics of deliverability in a nutshell.
