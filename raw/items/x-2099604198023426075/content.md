Title: The ULTIMATE AI UGC Playbook
URL: https://x.com/i/article/2099539880711950337

4 months from now, almost all of the UGC you see pushing apps WON'T have a real creator behind it.

 

and its not because creators suddenly got worse AI just gave faceless content the one thing it was always missing 

a personality

a believable face, expressions, body language, voice and environment

and unlike a real creator, that person can be duplicated across 20 accounts switched into another language even moved into another room / environment and given 30 different hooks in a few minutes

faceless accounts were always easier to scale , face creators were usually better at making people trust the content

AI is starting to give you both and I think a lot of app founders are still looking at this way too simply

they generate one AI girl and create a script when the video fails they decide AI UGC isnt worth it or dosent work

the interesting part starts when you stop making individual videos and build a system around formats that already work.

let's be honest: all ugc is fake

 


every single format of UGC is someone pretending to be an organic user and promote a product

the face creator showing off a product isn't doing it naturally instead theyre following a script provided to them

the “I just found this app” video was planned the screen recordings you see are planned

UGC has always been advertising made to feel real . so I don't really care whether the person on screen technically exists I care whether somebody stops.

wether they watch the video 

wether they understand the product

and most importantly wether they actually do whatever the video or the CTA was supposed to make them do 


none of it is real user-generated content. it's manufactured content designed to look organic. and its always been that way.

once you accept this the question stops being "which type is more authentic?"

it becomes: "which type performs better at scale?"

you can get a video to 10M views and still have shit conversions if the people watching don't care about the product

before making anything :

work backward from the customer.

-> what happens after they watch your video? 
-> where do they go? 
-> what does the video need to make them understand before they get there?
-> what problem are they already trying to solve?

face creators had one massive advantage

 

every face creator had one thing going for them and that was their identity. you could see someone's face hear their voice get eye contact, expressions, movement, little imperfections 

and that felt personal brands paid more for that

scaling faceless creators had its bottlenecks like 

-> one face 
-> one language 
-> maybe 3-5 videos a day
-> the cost per video was insane

faceless creators have always more scalable  you could run 50-100 accounts without burning out a personal 

but you gave up some of that human feel that made face UGC work so well

there was a massive trust gap between ai generated content and actual human generated content 

and fortunately that gap has been long closed by the recent advancment in ai models like Fable 5.1 , GPT-6 Astra and many more 

when the gap closed it didnt just come close to Face content it actually surpassed it in every way possible 

faceless creators didn't just gain the ability to show a face.

they gained the ability to show:  

-> any face
-> any language
-> any environment 
-> any gender 
-> any scale

you can build the whole AI UGC pipeline yourself.

or you can plug into creator distribution and let a shit ton of people test content for you.

both have very different advantages and ive explained both in detail

if you're comfortable with organic content, I'd probably start here but before generating anything, do some actual research

how to turn one winning AI UGC ad into 100

 

say you're marketing a calorie app and you find a video doing numbers. it's just a girl in her kitchen opening  hook with “apparently I've been eating way more calories than I thought”, she talks for a few seconds, opens the app, quick CTA and that's it.

don't copy the video word for word. figure out : 
-> why it worked
-> what happened in the first 3 seconds
-> when the app showed up
-> what she was doing while she talked
-> what problem the hook introduced 
-> what the product actually did before the CTA

 

once you have that structure, remake it around your own product with AI. maybe the customer is someone trying to lose weight, the hook is a casual confession, the setting is still a kitchen and the payoff is them scanning a meal and realizing where the extra calories are coming from.

then start making versions off that same idea. different hook, different creator, different room, different pain point, different product reveal, different language.

don't change everything at once either. if the same hook works with 3 different creators, keep pushing the hook. if people keep dropping before the app appears, show it earlier.

that's how one good UGC ad turns into 20-50 actual tests instead of 20 completely random AI videos 

once you start doing this properly, ideas aren't really the bottleneck anymore.
