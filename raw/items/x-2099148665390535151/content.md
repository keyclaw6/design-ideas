I had a chat a week ago with the founder of a Series B AI native CRM (raised $20M+).

They're taking over their market right now, and he shared an outbound playbook worth passing on.

Step 1. Define the TAM broadly.

Step 2. They figure out which CRM each company in the TAM uses. 

Their trick: job postings. 

Sales job listings often say things like "HubSpot experience required" or "Salesforce proficiency preferred." 

Scraping these maps out each company's CRM with surprising accuracy.

Step 3. Tailor outreach to their current CRM. 

HubSpot users get "Why switching from HubSpot to [their CRM] is a no brainer for your sales team." 
Same for Salesforce, Pipedrive, etc.

Step 4. Cold call everyone, and mention the CRM they're using. Instantly shows they did their homework.

Step 5. Conversion rates go way up compared to blasting the TAM blindly.

You can basically automate this whole thing with @GojiberryAI (and track which companies use which tech). 

It tracks companies by tech stack without scraping job posts, finds the right people, and launches outreach from there.
