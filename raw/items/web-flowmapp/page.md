# Flowmapp

**URL:** https://www.flowmapp.com/

Visual platform for planning web design projects: **brief, sitemap, structure, wireframes, content, estimation, invoice** — for agencies, studios, UX/UI designers, freelancers, developers.

## For agencies

- Increase design revenue, raise average check

| Step | Feature | Claim |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Brief, sitemap, AI generator, sticky notes | +15% in sales |
| 3 | Project cost, timing, dev requirements, invoice | +35% in sales |
| 4 | Proposal, export to Figma, PDF/PNG/XML/CSV, presentation | +50% in sales |

## Features

- **Brief** — project brief inside Flowmapp for sales proposals
- **Sitemap** — interactive visual sitemap with page editor, AI generator, collaboration
- **AI-powered generator** — sitemaps and wireframes from prompts
- **Sticky notes** — canvas comments and team collaboration
- **Wireframes** — generated alongside sitemap
- **Content** — headlines and copy on pages
- **User flows** — journey mapping
- **Estimation & invoice** — rates, hours, per-page breakdown; downloadable invoice
- **Export** — Figma, PDF, PNG, XML, CSV, webpage
- **Presentation** — interactive client presentation mode

## 9-step sales pipeline (site narrative)
