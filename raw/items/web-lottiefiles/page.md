# LottieFiles

**URL:** https://lottiefiles.com/

Platform for lightweight animations across websites, apps, presentations, and social. Trusted by 16M+ designers/developers from 280k+ companies.

## Core value props

- **Tiny file format** — dotLottie 90% smaller than GIF
- **Motion Tokens** — bind properties to real data
- **Interactive** animations for delightful experiences
- **Dev ready** — CDN, runtimes, VSCode integration

## Products

- **Lottie Creator** — AI Motion Copilot for ultra-lightweight customizable animations
- **Integrations** — Figma, After Effects, Canva, Webflow, Framer, Premiere Pro
- **DAM** — manages Lottie, dotLottie, PSD, AI, images; video support coming
- **AI features** — Motion Copilot, Prompt to Vector, Raster to Vector, Prompt to Themes

## Library

800,000+ free & premium ready-to-use animations searchable by category (loading, success, confetti, AI, etc.).
