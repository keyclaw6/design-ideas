## Post

@MichaelSaruggia

I’m sending 50,000 cold emails per month.

One ICP.
One offer.
About 1 hour of work per week.

Here is the 7-step system:

1. Start with real intent

I target:

- Followers of competitor LinkedIn pages
- People engaging with industry-related content

They already know the category.

2. Find relevant posts

Search LinkedIn for posts mentioning keywords connected to your offer.

For me:

- GTME
- GTM engineer
- Go-to-market engineering
- Clay

3. Scrape the engagers

Collect everyone liking or commenting on those posts.

A person engaging with GTME content is far more relevant than a random person with the right job title.

4. Qualify the ICP

Filter by role, geography, and relevance.

My current focus is people in Europe and the US who already understand GTM engineering.

5. Enrich and launch

My stack:

- Codex
- Deepline
- LinkedIn post search
- Harvest API
- Smartlead

Find the people, enrich their emails, and send qualified leads directly into Smartlead.

6. Automate the campaign

Save the workflow on GitHub and deploy it on Railway.

Run it every 24–48 hours so new posts and engagers continuously enter the campaign.

7. Treat cold email like an ad network

I want to reach my entire TAM every 30–90 days with a different message.

Sometimes it’s an offer.

Sometimes it’s a case study, video, or lead magnet.

The goal isn’t only meetings.

It’s becoming famous inside your target market.

Outbound creates inbound.

Inbound makes outbound warmer.

You don’t need everyone to know you.

You need your TAM to know you.
