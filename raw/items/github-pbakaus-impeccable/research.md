# Research

## What it is

Paul Bakaus’s Impeccable: design guidance for coding agents — one skill, 23 slash commands, live browser iteration, 61 deterministic detector rules. Evolved from Anthropic’s frontend-design skill. Site: impeccable.style.

## How it works

- `npx impeccable install` then `/impeccable init` writes PRODUCT.md (audience, voice, constraints) separately from DESIGN.md (visual direction).
- Commands: `document` (DESIGN.md from code), `craft`, `shape`, `critique`, `audit`, `polish`, `animate`, `bolder`, `quieter`, `distill`, `live` (browser variants).
- Detectors are deterministic (no LLM) against Inter-everywhere, purple-blue gradients, card-in-card, and other slop tells; LLM critique sits on top.
- Combines Anthropic frontend-design lineage with Google DESIGN.md as the durable visual file.

## Why saved

Splits product truth from visual system — missing from extract-only DESIGN.md tools. Best “don’t ship AI purple” operating system in the bank.
