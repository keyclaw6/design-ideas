## Post

cold email explained

Low IQ:

Get a list of people with the problem. Send them four sentences about the problem and one specific offer. Send a lot of them.

Midwit:

40 domains, 120 mailboxes, a rotation schedule, and a spreadsheet tracking mailbox health
Warmup tools running permanently at 30 emails/day forever
Spintax on every sentence, {{first_name|there}} fallbacks nested three deep
"Spam word" avoidance — rewriting "free" and "guarantee" out of copy like it's 2011 Gmail
Open tracking on, via a custom tracking domain (open tracking is itself a deliverability liability)
AI personalization on the wrong variable: "Noticed you studied at Michigan State — go Spartans!"
9-step sequences ending in "bumping this up," "did this get buried?", and the breakup email
14 micro-ICP segments with bespoke copy for each
A/B testing subject lines on 200 sends and declaring a winner
Reporting reply rate as the KPI
When it doesn't work: buy more data, add more mailboxes

High IQ:

Precise list, short email, one specific offer, high volume, hygiene done once. Measure meetings held and pipeline, nothing above it.
