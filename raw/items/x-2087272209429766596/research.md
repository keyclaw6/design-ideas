# Research

## What it is
CadXStudio demo: one prompt yields a full parametric bookshelf-speaker enclosure (walnut legs, perforated grille), claimed manufacturing-ready, in-browser at https://cadxstudio.in — no traditional CAD UI.

## How it works
- Text-to-CAD: LLM → parametric model (enclosure, legs, grille).
- Marketing claim: no CAD years required; idea → geometry.
- Same product family as other CadX bookmarks (wheel hub, screwdriver, Stanley cup).
- Video/screenshot on disk is the generated speaker.

## Why saved
Hardware-adjacent generative CAD. Not keyboard-PCB-specific, but the same “prompt a manufacturable part” motion as later Fusion/OpenSCAD items. Useful if BESS needs custom enclosures or fixtures.
