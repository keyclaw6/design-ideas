BLENDER LOCKS THE SHOT. SEEDANCE FINISHES IT

This is not a lucky night video.

The 3D scene was built first.
Then the camera was locked inside Blender.

Higgsfield plugin sits inside Blender.

Low-poly car. Road. Exact camera path.
That previz becomes the structure for Seedance 2.5

Then the refs go in:
> location for the night look 
> photos for the two characters 
> photos for the car

Seedance follows the Blender move. 
Same angles. Same timing. Same path.

The prompt does not invent the scene.
It locks it:
> no new shots 
> no swapped seats 
> no falling rain

The raw 3D scene is the skeleton. 
Seedance only puts the skin on.
