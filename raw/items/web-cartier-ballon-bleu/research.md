# Research

## What it is

Cartier DK product page for Ballon Bleu CRWSBB0049 — kept as a luxury e-commerce UX/motion reference, not as a watch-buying note.

## How it works

- Spec table: 42 mm, caliber 1847 MC, steel, guilloché dial, 40h reserve, 3 bar WR.
- UX extras: wrist-measure bracelet fit (125–190 mm), engraving personalization, animated user guide (time set, bracelet, chronograph).
- High-end PDP patterns: materials storytelling, configurators, motion as instruction rather than decoration.
- Logistics: express delivery, 30-day returns (DK market).
- Use as craft ceiling for product-detail pages (BESS hardware, energy products) that need explainer motion.

## Why saved

Reference for how luxury brands teach a physical object on the web with motion and personalization. Complements Utsubo/Oryzo experiential sites at the PDP grain.
