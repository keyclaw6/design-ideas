- I believe autoresearch matters because it turns manual experimentation into a continuous loop where agents propose changes, run experiments, evaluate results, and learn from each attempt.
- I find Meta-Harness especially compelling because it extends this idea beyond model training to the entire system around an LLM, including its prompts, retrieval, memory, tools, state, parsers, and control flow.
- My primer on autoresearch and meta-harness presents a practical, end-to-end view of building autonomous research systems, from single-GPU code optimization to trace-driven harness search, multi-objective evaluation, safety controls, and scaled multi-agent research loops.
