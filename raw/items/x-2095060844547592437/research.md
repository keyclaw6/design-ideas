## What they are actually doing

Paolo Trivellato (founder, Starborn) is teaching the **distribution layer** of LinkedIn lead-magnet posts, not how to build the PDF/Notion asset. **Three levers as written**

1. **Image = thumbnail.** One idea, proof screenshot (revenue, booked calendar, resource preview), contrast. He attributes most underperformance to a weak image (stated as 95%).
2. **Hook = specific result + open question.** Example in the post: “I booked 672 demos in 30 days using LinkedIn.” Same 672-demo figure appears as a Starborn case study (Prosp.ai / Yann Dine) on the site.
3. **Body = preview lines + comment machine.** Pain in buyer language; “internal” framing; effort/scarcity; CTA one word, &lt;6 letters; link lives in DMs/comments, not the post.

The X banner is a thin branded strip, not the LinkedIn thumbnail he describes.
