## Capture notes

- Desktop Windows tool from Arcana Mfg.; bridges photogrammetry/splat captures to printable/edit meshes.
- Promoted via @ymt3d X post (capture-6); zip URL recorded but not stored (110 MB).
- PLY in → OBJ/GLB out; sample data offered on download page.

## BESS / 3D flythrough relevance

Useful when BESS or site marketing needs physical models or DCC assets from Gaussian splat scans of plants, buildings, or terrain — splats alone cannot print or import cleanly into Blender/game engines.
