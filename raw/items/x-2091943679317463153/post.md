**Author:** Spenser Dickerson (@SpenserFX)
**URL:** https://x.com/SpenserFX/status/2091943679317463153

SplatPaint is a real-time browser-based creative sandbox for Gaussian Splats: paint, sculpt, recolor, relight, deform, animate, and layer wicked particle system FX.

You don’t even need a splat asset to begin. Grab an image, logo, or 3D model, convert it into splats (inside SplatPaint), and start building something unique.

For the next 48 hours, SplatPaint is open to everyone for free.
