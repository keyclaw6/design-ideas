“GDPR”
“GDPR compliance”
“SOC 2”
“data privacy”
“security compliance”

step 2: monitor everyone in your ICP engaging with content around those keywords

the second someone fits the ICP + interacts with one of those topics

the system catches them

step 3: automatically filter the lead

right role?
right company size?
right geography?
actual potential buyer?

if yes, keep them

if no, ignore them

step 4: enrich the lead

find the email
find the phone number
get the LinkedIn profile
pull the company context

now you have a fresh list of people showing actual intent every single day

step 5: contact them while the signal is still fresh

email them
DM them on LinkedIn
or just call

but that’s only one part of the system

step 6: monitor every competitor

he tracks posts from:

competitor company pages
competitor founders
competitor salespeople
competitor employees

if someone in his ICP engages with one of those posts

he knows

someone repeatedly interacting with a GDPR vendor is giving you a pretty obvious signal

step 7: monitor funding rounds

he tracks companies that just raised a big Seed or Series A

because he knows what happens next:

the company grows
enterprise deals get bigger
security questionnaires start showing up
customers start asking about compliance
GDPR / SOC 2 suddenly become urgent

that’s when he calls

step 8: stack the signals

someone fits your ICP = interesting

someone fits your ICP + engages with “GDPR compliance” = better

someone fits your ICP + engages with compliance content + follows your competitors = very good

someone does all of that AND just raised a Series A?

call them now
