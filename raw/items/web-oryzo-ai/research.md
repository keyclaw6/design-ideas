# Research

## What it is

oryzo.ai: satirical high-production site by Lusion presenting a cork coaster as an over-engineered “AI product” (Oryzo-1 open-weight 3D coaster). Parodies AI landing tropes (model cards, fake arXiv, encryption gags) with real interactive 3D/hover craft.

## How it works

- Hero copy, fake SKUs (ORYZO / Pro / Pro Max as stack height), “smart flip encryption,” antislip/thermodynamic jokes, peer-review block, magazine issue mock.
- Interactive 3D demos; audio-forward/experiential pacing typical of Lusion.
- Not a product to buy — a craft and comedy reference for what award-studio WebGL looks like when roasting AI slop.
- Complements Utsubo (serious experiential studio) and Opale (taste essay).

## Why saved

Calibrates the ceiling for 3D marketing sites and lampoons the same tropes scroll-craft’s refuse list targets. Use as “this is the bar / this is the joke.”
