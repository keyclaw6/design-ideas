# Research

## What it is
Top3D.ai arena drop: Hitem3D v3.0 (preview) and Meshy 7 in the main arena, Tripo P2.0 in the low-poly arena, same prompt pool and max settings, ranked by blind battles.

## How it works
- Blind A/B of image-to-3D / text-to-3D vendors under matched prompts.
- Arena URL: https://www.top3d.ai/arena
- Hitem3D / Meshy / Tripo are mesh generators — input for web 3D, splat cleanup, or BESS asset blockouts (equipment, buildings) before a flythrough or Three.js hero.
- Low-poly track (Tripo P2.0) is the relevant one for marketing-page budgets; main arena is higher-fidelity / heavier.
- Use as a living bake-off instead of trusting vendor reels.

## Why saved
BESS flythroughs and Three.js landings need mesh sources. An independent arena is how to pick Meshy vs Tripo vs Hitem without a paid bake-off.
