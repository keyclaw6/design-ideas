GITHUB IS FULL OF TOOLS FOR GETTING TRAFFIC

Went through hundreds of GitHub repos looking for open-source tools that can actually help with getting and automating traffic.

Here’s what I found worth digging into.

> ALL-IN-ONE

• MoneyPrinterV2
   http://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/MoneyPrinterV2

The author literally describes it as a tool for automating online income. It comes with 4 different modules:

1. YouTube Shorts - LLM writes the script, Nano Banana generates the visuals, music gets added, and the video is uploaded to your channel on schedule.

2. Twitter bot - create an account, set a topic, and let it generate and post content on a schedule.

3. Affiliate marketing - pulls products from Amazon, LLM writes a salesy tweet and publishes it.

4. Client hunting - scrapes local businesses from Google Maps and sends cold emails.

You can also push the generated Shorts to TikTok and Instagram.

My take: the repo is definitely a bit overhyped, but there’s actually some solid functionality in there if you know how to use and modify it.

> PINTEREST

• tiktok-to-pinterest
   http://github.com/mehdreaming/tiktok-to-pinterest

An n8n workflow that finds viral TikToks, uses AI to turn them into Pinterest pins and puts everything into a queue.

• pinterest-python-sdk
   http://github.com/pinterest/pinterest-python-sdk

Official Pinterest SDK. You need API access, but it’s a much cleaner way to automate things without relying on random bots.

> SHORTS

• AI-Youtube-Shorts-Generator
   http://github.com/Anil-matcha/AI-Youtube-Shorts-Generator

Basically a free Opus Clip alternative.

Drop in a long video, AI finds the best moments and turns them into vertical clips without watermarks.

• MoneyPrinterTurbo
   http://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo

Generates short videos from a topic with almost everything handled for you: script, voiceover, footage and subtitles.

Think of it as a lighter version of the all-in-one repo above.

> THREADS

• claude-skill-social-post
   http://github.com/Hao0321/claude-skill-social-post

A Claude skill that can write posts in your style and publish them across Threads, Instagram and Facebook.

> REDDIT

• Reddit-Copilot
   http://github.com/meet447/Reddit-Copilot

Describe your product and it finds Reddit threads where people are looking for something similar or complaining about a problem you solve.

Then it writes a draft reply for you.
You post it manually.

Why manually? Reddit tends to ban this kind of automation pretty fast. But you can fork it and build your own workflow around it.

> X / TWITTER

• XActions
   http://github.com/nirholas/XActions

A collection of X tools with an MCP server. Connect it to your AI agent and let it work with your account.

• x-engage
   http://github.com/dancolta/x-engage

Give it a list of accounts and topics you care about and it finds 5-15 posts where you could jump into the conversation right now.

It can also generate the replies for you.

> INSTAGRAM

• instagram-skills
   http://github.com/sergebulaev/instagram-skills

9 Claude Code skills for captions, Reels hooks, carousels and content planning in your own voice.
Everything goes through your approval before publishing.

It doesn’t fully create everything from scratch, but it can speed up the boring parts of content production quite a lot.

> FACEBOOK

• facebook-skills
   http://github.com/sergebulaev/facebook-skills

Same author, but for Facebook.

Posts, comment analysis and weekly content planning.

Facebook still has a lot of organic reach, and the competition is different from Instagram and X.

I’ve also heard from people I know that simply cross-posting their content there can sometimes bring surprisingly more traffic than Instagram + X combined.

> TIKTOK / YOUTUBE UPLOADS

• social-auto-upload
   http://github.com/dreammis/social-auto-upload

Automatically uploads videos to TikTok, YouTube and a bunch of other platforms.

Haven’t tested this one myself. TikTok automation can get messy, so I’d test it on an account you don’t mind losing first.

> IMPORTANT

I couldn’t find a public repo that lets you turn it on once and magically get unlimited traffic with zero work.
And honestly, that makes sense.

Most of these are building blocks, not a giant GET TRAFFIC button.

The fun part is combining them.

Fork a repo → connect your APIs → change the logic → plug a few tools together → build your own workflow.

That’s where things get interesting.

Save this one. There’s a lot to dig through.
