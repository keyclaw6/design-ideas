LightFuse: Relightable Interactive Gaussian Scene Reconstruction via Multi-Scan Fusion and 2D Gaussian Ray Tracing

Contributions:
1. We present LightFuse, to our knowledge the first framework for relightable multi-scan interactive Gaussian reconstruction with explicit material–illumination decomposition.

2. We propose ray-tracing-oriented geometry optimization with coverage, scale, depth, and normal regularization to improve surface consistency under multi-state joint supervision.

3. We develop staged inverse rendering to separate shared material from state-specific environment illumination. The resulting physically consistent relighting achieves state-of-the-art quality across synthetic scenes, surpassing the strongest baseline by +9.74 dB PSNR and +0.121 SSIM on average.
