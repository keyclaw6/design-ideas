# Research

## What it is

animos.app/editor: browser SPA (React/Vite) with 25 motion templates for design showcases — drop in images or video, tweak, export MP4/WebM locally in the browser.

## How it works

- Template-first: pick a showcase template, swap media, export.
- No After Effects or code; fonts Geist/Inter/JetBrains Mono.
- Consent-gated GA from app code, not head.
- Output is raster video, not Lottie/CSS — complementary to LottieFiles and motion-anything.
- Thin capture (meta + product description); editor internals not reverse-engineered.

## Why saved

Fast path for portfolio/case-study motion without a video model or AE. Useful for polishing stills (TinyShots) into a short loop for a landing or pitch.
