# animos Editor

**URL:** https://animos.app/editor

Browser-based motion template tool for design showcases.

## From meta tags

- **Tagline:** Motion templates for design showcases
- **Description:** 25 ready-made animation templates — drop in images or videos, tweak, and export MP4/WebM in your browser
- **Site:** animos.app

## Product

SPA (React/Vite) editor at `/editor`. Consent-gated Google Analytics loaded from app code, not head. Fonts: Geist, Inter, JetBrains Mono.

## Use case

Quick motion exports for portfolio/design presentation without After Effects or code.
