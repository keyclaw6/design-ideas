## Post

if you wanna master AI content, here’s the playbook:

- pick one image model and one video model. learn how they work. try different prompts, references, and settings. play around until you understand what changes the output

- make something every single day. a product shot, a short clip, a UGC ad, an animation, take it from idea to finished piece. save your best prompts and the exact steps in a google doc

- when something fails, try again. change something. try again. a bad output doesn’t mean it’s impossible, trial and error is how you learn what works

- once you’ve figured out a repeatable workflow, give your docs to codex or claude code. have it connect to your browser and walk through the process, fix the parts where it gets stuck

- keep refining until you can drop in a script and have your agent handle the full production process

- add video editing skills so your agent can put the entire ad together, and hand you an edited video ready for upload
