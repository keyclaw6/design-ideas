# Research

## What it is

Jakub Krehel (Interfaces magazine) MIT skill collection for building interfaces: UI, typography, color, accessibility, layout, and product writing — installable via `npx skills add jakubkrehel/skills`.

## How it works

- `better-interface` runs a combined review across the better-* family.
- Specialists: `better-ui` (concentric radius, optical alignment, hit areas, animation), `better-typography` (scale, variable fonts, OpenType, wrapping), `better-colors` (palettes, semantic tokens, contrast), `better-accessibility` (WCAG), `better-layout` (grouping, reading order).
- `interface-review` is a user-invoked multi-category audit; `variant` generates multiple component variants for iteration.
- Skills are review-oriented: they tell the agent what to check, not a component library to paste.
- Site: jakub.kr/skills; complements Emil/Taste/Impeccable which skew motion and anti-slop.

## Why saved

Coverage of type, contrast, and a11y that motion-first packs skip. Useful as a QA pass after scroll-craft or OpenDesign generate a landing.
