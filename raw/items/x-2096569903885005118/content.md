## Post

Here’s THE new viral format on LinkedIn 👇

You already know lead magnet posts with the CTA written directly on the image...

Now meet: GIFs.

The format is ridiculously simple:

→ Record a quick screen capture of your resource
→ Add a static CTA on top (with Claude or manually in iMovie)
→ Write a post that teases the resource without including the CTA in the text
→ Turn the screen recording into a GIF

That’s it.

We tested this against our usual static images and got 3x more impressions.

Simple change. Massive difference.

Good luck 🫡
