- Platforms named: ChatGPT, Google, Bing, Meta, Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Claude, Grok, Gemini.
- First 30 days: visibility baseline across Google + AI, then opportunity/effort/intent sprints — not a fixed content quota.
- Measures rankings, citations, share of answer → demand/pipeline where data allows. Explicitly does not guarantee AI placements.
- Book-a-call CTA; not a self-serve MCP (unlike CrowdReply).
