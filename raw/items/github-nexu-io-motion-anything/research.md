# Research

## What it is

nexu-io/motion-anything (~750 stars, Apache-2.0): open-source chat-native motion engine from the Open Design family. Describe motion in natural language; the agent generates and edits animations on the live page. Zero npm dependencies.

## How it works

- 403 curated recipes (kinetic text, GPU shaders, canvas) plus a component workbench: 4 triggers, 13 motion verbs, spring easing, keyframe editor.
- Eight coding-agent engines + BYOK (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, Grok, Hermes, Gemini, Open Design Cloud).
- Export JSON, CSS, React, Lottie, MP4, GIF, and portable skills.
- Integrates Open Design catalogs: 59 brand packs, 58 HyperFrames templates, 112 HTML prototypes, 2,680 icons.
- Modes: Home (one sentence → artifact), Workbench (live page + restraint-budget warnings), video editor (WebCodecs MP4, no watermark).

## Why saved

Motion recipes that export to Lottie/CSS/MP4 sit between Emil’s taste skills and html-video’s full film pipeline. Useful for landing micro-interactions without After Effects.
