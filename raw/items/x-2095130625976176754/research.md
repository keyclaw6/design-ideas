## What they are actually doing

Serg (blogr.ai, also larafast.com; sold directify.app) shipped **topical maps** into the paid SEO agent, and points to a free try-on-your-site version.

**Paid Blogr.ai** (`https://blogr.ai/`)

- Loop: research → plan → write → publish → optimize. Human-in-the-loop (approve titles) or Auto Publish.
- Connects Google Search Console; publishes to WordPress / Webflow / Shopify / webhooks.
- Map is built from a live keyword DB: current rankings, organic competitors, demand gap — not LLM-guessed keywords.
- Per page: monthly searches, difficulty, intent, current position, opportunity score; articles then written in map order.
- Claims: no cannibalization, internal links designed up front, long-tail coverage around the hub.
- Positioning: SaaS / ecommerce / local SEO. 7-day trial. Founders: engineer + SEO operator (Connor Showler mentioned as co-founder who introduced topical maps).

**Free Topical Map Generator** (`https://blogr.ai/tools/topical-map-generator`)

- Narrower than the paid map: reads the landing page, outputs **six pillars × five articles** (headline, target keyword, intent). Not the GSC/competitor/opportunity-score map described in the tweet.
- No account for the first pillar; email unlocks the rest + CSV.
