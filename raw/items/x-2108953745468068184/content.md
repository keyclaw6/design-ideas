that really depends on your budget, familiarity with different level of professional camera/audio equipment

this is a Canon C50 which is a prosumer XLR input for audio Canon RF (still photo lenses) setup.  Can record 4K for infinite time and works as a good webcam

OpenAI shoots Arri Alexa Mini (like $250K+ each setup) and dual professional audio, even for YouTube videos.

going down market, a Sony A7 would be fine

for ultra low budget and iPhone Pro Max or laptop webcam is usable in good lighting environments

generally microphone is the first purchase and then you start of figure out if you are XLR, dual audio, etc. and that starts to sort out what cameras pair best
