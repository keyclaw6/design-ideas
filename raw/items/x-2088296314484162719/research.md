# Research

## What it is
Rina (@irinatoxi) on why AI-in-CAD is bigger than text-to-3D: agents treating a messy Autodesk Fusion project as a database — search, isolate, param-edit, re-layout from English.

## How it works
- Demo (described): create a geometric structure, find fasteners in a large assembly, hide the rest, change dimensions across screw sizes, rearrange hardware — no browser-tree archaeology.
- Claim: MCP-style agents that understand existing engineering data automate the boring half of CAD (find, filter, param, pattern), not just the drawing half.
- Distinct from CadX/VibeCAD “prompt a new part”: this is query-and-mutate on a live assembly.
- Directly relevant to keyboard PCBs, racks, and BESS skid models that already exist as CAD.

## Why saved
KB’s hardware lane will inherit large assemblies. The winning interface is “SQL for CAD,” not another generator. Bookmark for Fusion/MCP experiments.
