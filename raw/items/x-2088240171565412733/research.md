# Research

## What it is
Gemini 3.7 Flash High in Antigravity generating a mechanically detailed Bugatti W16 engine in Three.js in ~4 minutes (accurate internals + photoreal exterior).

## How it works
- Antigravity = Google’s agent/IDE surface; model is Gemini 3.7 Flash High.
- Output is an interactive Three.js scene, not a video — camera can orbit a complex mechanical assembly.
- Quality bar for “can an agent model real machinery” (relevant to BESS inverters, racks, transformers).
- Pair with MengTo Three.js landing tutorials and graphics agent skills for productionizing the scene (load, lights, scroll camera).
- Treat as a capability demo, not a repo; reproduce with a tight mechanical brief + Three.js skills.

## Why saved
Proof that a fast Gemini agent can emit inspectable mechanical 3D for marketing, not just blobs. Calibration for BESS equipment heroes.
