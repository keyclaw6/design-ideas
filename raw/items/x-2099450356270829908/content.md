[I built a $250M company in 3 months. Here's every idea I tried before it worked:](https://x.com/i/article/2099448117561016320)

Cover: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HSK9FDzXIAAi_E2.jpg

I’ve been an entrepreneur for 20+ years. 

Most of my businesses failed, but a few of them changed my life. 

If you want to win, keep trying even when it’s hard.

---

1. Moved to New York at 21

I studied financial engineering at Columbia. 

I wanted to be a founder but didn’t have the guts.

---

2. Joined a startup hedge fund on Wall Street

I wanted to learn how things get built from scratch.

 It died in a year.

---

3. Joined Barclays Capital

They had just bought Lehman Brothers. 

I worked on the trading floor for 2 years. 

Had a lot of friends building software back then, and I started to feel inspired.

---

4. Learned how to code

My best friend told me that once I learned, I could build anything. 

So I did. Nights and weekends. For 6 months straight.

---

5. Shipped my first app

Did it under the alias Ben Cera so my finance boss wouldn’t find out. 

People loved it. I quit my job, bootstrapped the company for a year… it failed.

---

6. Built a photo messaging app

Josh Kushner from Thrive Capital loved it and invested $1 million from his brand-new fund.

---

7. Pivoted into a people discovery app

It had massive traction. 

But then I realized I was building a dating app and decided to walk away.

---

8. Moved to LA

I became the CTO of a tech-enabled interior design startup. 

We raised $20M from Founders Fund and Zillow Group.

---

9. Worked with Uber's founder

Travis Kalanick bought CloudKitchens and I got a meeting. 

I decided to join as a product manager. 

2 years later, I was leading a new division I had built from scratch.

---

10. Decided to come back to the arena

I moved to Paris to start a new company called GiftShop with a good friend, and we raised $8M. 

After 2 years of co-founder tension, I finally walked away to go solo.

---

11. Started Polsia Inc

Decided that I wanted to start working with AI right away, but also take a break and see the world. 

So I started traveling the world and touring most of Asia.

---

12. Built Blanks

An iPhone app that builds iPhone apps using AI. Failed.

---

13. Built Decision AI

An AI that makes decisions for you. Zero customers.

---

14. Started Polsia

I went all in on the idea of building a company that builds and runs companies with AI. 

I built the first version and moved to San Francisco.

---

15. Polsia worked

5 months later, I had 10,000+ customers, $10M run rate, and raised $30M at a $250M valuation. 

This is over 20 years of building, failing, and starting over. 

Every lesson is tuition. Nothing is ever wasted.
