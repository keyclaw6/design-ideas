## What it is

Pointer to iCraft Editor (gantFDT/icraft): a tool for 3D network/architecture diagrams with cinematic visual effects.

## How it works

- GitHub: github.com/gantFDT/icraft — 3D diagram editor, not a generic Three.js boilerplate.
- Use case: infra/network maps that need depth, glow, and camera rather than 2D boxes.
- Relevant when BESS/site electrical one-lines or agent-system diagrams should look like product, not Lucidchart.
- Sits with AntV/Flint (2D infographic) as the 3D diagram option.
- Tweet is a one-liner; evaluate the repo for export (GLB/web) before committing a pipeline.

## Why saved

KB needs architecture diagrams that can live on a marketing site or in a 3D hero, not only mermaid.
