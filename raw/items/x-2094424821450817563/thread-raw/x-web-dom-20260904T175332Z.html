The best teams in GTM understood one thing: Many personal brands = company brand. For context... A B2B deal in 2026 gets decided by 13 internal stakeholders and 9 outside influencers. No single founder account can reach all 22, but a team posting across every department is able to easily. That's how we book 80+ meetings a month from content. So I put the entire system into a free playbook: The Employee-Led Content Playbook. Inside, I break down all 5 stages: • The 5 stages: company content strategy, personal brand, company page, creation, conversion • The stakeholder math: a 2026 B2B deal decided by 13 internal + 9 outside stakeholders • How each teammate owns 3-4 content pillars mapped to a department • Why a personal profile out-reaches the company page (3,000+ people vs about 500) • The creation stack that removes blockers (Notion, Ergo, GitHub Company OS, Pinecone with 2,000+ viral posts) • The 5-layer conversion funnel (Jungler, RB2B, Tally, then Clay, HubSpot, Instantly(.)ai, HeyReach, Nooks, Cal(.)com) • When it works: starts at 3-4 posters, takes off at 10+ Comment "TEAM" and I'll DM it to you.
