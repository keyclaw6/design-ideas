**Product (3dnchu + Arcana HTML):** Windows 11, local, **GPU not required**. PLY 3DGS in → OBJ/GLB out. Personal / non-commercial free; commercial use of the app **or** of converted meshes needs a paid license. Use cases on the vendor page: 3D print, Blender edit, large-scale scan, game/sim. YouTube embeds: `CwtxJJTOV28`, `7LH-aloguBU`.

## Fit for BESS / 3D marketing

Turns a Gaussian splat (site/plant scan) into an editable mesh without a GPU box. Closer to “get the splat into Blender/GLB” than novel-view diffusion (ArtiFixer).
