# Research

## What it is
CadX Studio text-to-CAD demo: a hexacopter generated because “CAD felt like a wall.” Built in CadX (tweet URL typo `cadxstduio.in`; canonical is https://cadxstudio.in).

## How it works
- Prompt-to-parametric hardware; sibling posts in the library do a bookshelf and a screwdriver on the same product.
- Barrier-removal pitch: hardware ideas die at CAD UX; agent CAD is the new sketch.
- Not a substitute for Fusion-as-database (editing existing assemblies) — this is greenfield generation.
- For keyboards/enclosures, use as a speed sketch, then rebuild in OpenSCAD/Fusion for manufacturing constraints.

## Why saved
Third CadX example (drone vs furniture vs tool) showing the product is general text-to-CAD, not a one-off bookshelf generator.
