# MeiGen

**URL:** https://www.meigen.ai/

## What it is

Free AI prompts gallery for image and video generation. Browse, search, and publish community prompts across GPT Image 2, Seedance, Nanobanana (Gemini), and Midjourney. Includes image-to-prompt, character reuse, and a generation UI with quality tiers (Standard / Medium / High, Auto 2K).
