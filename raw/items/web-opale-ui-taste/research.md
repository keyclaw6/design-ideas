# Research

## What it is

Opale UI blog essay by Elyes Rayane Melbouci: a developer’s guide to taste in the age of AI. Argues against downloading generic skills; encode *your* likes/hates into SKILL.md plus bold persona prompts. “Taste is scalable.”

## How it works

- Diagnoses “vibe coder aesthetic”: purple gradients, rectangular grids, emoji as decoration.
- Practice: when you hate something, write why in skills; when you love something, capture the reasoning — iterative refinement, not one-shot packs.
- Cited personal work: 3D brain assembling on scroll, CSS @property cards, WebGL liquid, scroll-linked spring text, noise field, magnetic cursor, defense-tech radar/globe, chat UI with WebGL orb.
- Complements Taste Skill / Emil (shared packs) with a *personalization* method.

## Why saved

Method for making skill packs actually yours — critical if BESS marketing must not look like every other AI landing. Three.js/ui-motion tags from the examples, not a library.
