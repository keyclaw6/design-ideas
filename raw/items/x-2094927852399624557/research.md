## What they are actually doing

The post is a capacity formula for B2B cold email, not a product demo. Volume is framed as mailbox and domain count, then two tools: Instantly as the send/sequence layer, MapsData as the lead list.

**Stack as stated**

- **500 inboxes / 167 domains** → ~3 inboxes per domain, ~20 sends/inbox/day if 10k/day is literal (10,000 / 500 = 20). The post does not spell out warmup, ESP mix, or copy.
- **[Instantly.ai](https://instantly.ai)** — cold-email sequencer: unlimited mailboxes positioning, warmup/deliverability, campaign AI (leads, copy, reply agent), CRM/pipeline. Schema.org line: “Cold email software for scaling outreach campaigns with unlimited email accounts and AI-powered lead generation.” Levi tags it only as “sequencer.”
- **[MapsData](https://mapsdata.ai)** — live Google Maps scraper for US/CA/UK/AU. Maps listings have no emails; they crawl each business site, verify addresses, export CSV/XLSX. Free tier 500 leads/month; paid from ~$19/mo; they advertise ~$3.30 per 10k leads-with-email on the top plan. Author’s X profile website is mapsdata.ai; in-thread he says he has sent to millions of MapsData leads and that it works for **businesses under 50 employees**.

Levi Munneke, B2B outbound; site b2bfunnel.co. The post is infrastructure arithmetic plus a two-vendor stack, not a case study with reply/meeting rates.
