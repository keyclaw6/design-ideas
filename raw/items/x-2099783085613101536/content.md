HERE IS THE FULL SYSTEM THAT BOOKS 20+ QUALIFIED CALLS A MONTH FROM LINKEDIN:

If you are posting on LinkedIn and booking zero calls then read this NOW. If you bookmark it you aren't going to take action on it. Okay let's go.

Hour 1: Content and lead magnets

Fix the profile before you post anything. Headline = who you help + outcome + timeframe. Custom button goes straight to Calendly with qualifying questions. About section ends in a one word DM CTA. Featured section has your best result and your best lead magnet pinned. This takes 2 hours once and it's the reason most content generates impressions and zero calls.

Post one piece of content every single day including weekends. Rotate between lead magnets, authority posts, case studies, and setup call posts. Map every post to an awareness stage before you write it. Problem unaware, pain aware, solution aware. Never post randomly and call it a content strategy.

Run two lead magnets a week. One word CTA under 6 letters, connection required, no link in the post body. Reply to every single comment within 2 hours or the momentum dies. The algorithm uses the first 90 minutes as the primary signal for how far to push the post.

Hour 2: DMs and follow-ups

Every commenter gets a DM within 60 seconds via Leadshark automation. Opt-in page only, never the resource directly - ask for name and email, one field, resource delivers automatically. The page redirects to a VSL the moment they submit.

Run the full DM sequence on every commenter, every profile visitor, every new connection, every liker on competitor posts. Resource first no pitch. Qualifying question 24 hours later. Case study once they respond. Specific time and date once fit is confirmed. Five messages maximum.

Never let a warm lead go more than 5 days without a touchpoint. The window closes faster than most people think.

Hour 3: Distribution and compounding

Repurpose every LinkedIn post to X the same day. All lowercase, shorter sentences, more direct. 10 minutes per post, two channels for zero extra content.

Add early engagement from 2-3 real connections in the first 30 minutes of every post going live. The algorithm uses early velocity as the primary signal.

Run call transcripts through Claude weekly. Extract every objection, hesitation, and question. Each one becomes next week's content. 

Build the email list behind every lead magnet. Name and email, resource delivers automatically, welcome sequence of 5 emails over 7 days. LinkedIn is a rented audience. The email list is the only asset you actually own.

After 30 days of running all three hours consistently you will have more inbound than you know what to do with. If you haven't booked a single call DM me. But you will.

Now if 3 hours a day is too much, here's the 30 minute version that still works, just slower:

- Post one lead magnet twice a week
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours
- Send the resource via DM manually
- Follow up once with a qualifying question

You'll get results. Slower, smaller, but real. The difference between 30 minutes and 3 hours is the difference between 5 calls a month and 50.
