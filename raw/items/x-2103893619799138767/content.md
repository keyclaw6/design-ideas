## Post

i find it laughable when people say that “AI finally killed cold email”

this is the WIDEST OPEN i've seen in outbound since 2020. 

our agency is now sending 10M+ cold emails/mo

and are currently scaling that up fast. 

what changed? 

> anyone can generate fake personalization for free now.
> so every agency runs the same "hey {first_name}, saw you're the {title}" line.
> prospects find this “personalization” utterly repulsive. 

all of this messaging comes from the same 3 tools.

and everyone is panicking about THAT.

i say: 

good.

let them panic.

while they buy another tool to sound clever, the boring stuff is sitting wide open:

• a list narrowed until it describes one exact human
• an offer good enough that can get a cold stranger to reply
• a reply that’s worked in minutes 
• a second and third in combination with the email

all of this became 10x rarer when AI showed up. 

no one’s stopping you from doing it 🤷
