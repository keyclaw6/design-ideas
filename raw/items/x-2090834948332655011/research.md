## What it is

Crowd ranking of ten installable anti-slop skills (skills.sh), from stop-slop to anti-ai-slop-writing.

## How it works

- Named packs: stop-slop, no-ai-slop, humanizer (two authors), unslop, slopbeth, deslop, anti-slop, humanize, anti-ai-slop-writing.
- Mix of prose humanizers and code/UI anti-template rules — install the one that matches the failure mode.
- Complements Taste Skill, unlazy, Google style-guide skill, and Impeccable rather than replacing them.
- Use as a shopping list; do not enable all ten (conflicting voice rules).
- Good default: one writing humanizer + one visual/code anti-slop (taste or impeccable) + unlazy.

## Why saved

KB's whole catalog is anti-slop for BESS/marketing agents; this is the index of community skills.
