ABOUT 3DGS What is 3DGS? 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) is a technique that represents real-world objects and environments using a large number of Gaussian primitives. It can create highly realistic 3D representations from photographs, but unlike conventional 3D models, 3DGS data does not consist of polygonal surfaces.
