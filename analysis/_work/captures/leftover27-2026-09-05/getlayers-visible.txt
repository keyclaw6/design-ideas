Yes — a curated set of free layers, full prompt included, no card. Copy one into your AI and you will know within a few minutes whether this is for you. That is what it is there for. How do I actually use a prompt? Press Copy prompt, paste it into whatever AI writes your code — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot, Lovable, Bolt, v0 — and it recreates the layer. Pair that with our Next.js + Claude starter , or with the AI design vault if you are not on Next.js — it is stack-agnostic, so you can build in any framework — and that is where the AI design superpowers actually start. Will it work with my stack? Yes. A prompt is plain structured language, not code, so it is tied to no framework and no vendor. It builds a self-contained HTML file by default; add one line and your AI builds it in Next.js, Astro, Vue, WordPress, whatever you are already in. Full Stack ships a real Next.js project if you would rather start from working code. I do not see anything for my industry. You will not — and that is the point. A layer is a foundation, not a finished site for a sector: the motion, depth and craft are what you are taking, and the subject is yours to swap. Pick the one that feels right, then tell your AI to make it about your business. Search by feeling, not by niche. Can I change it, or am I stuck with the design? Change everything. Scenes and gradients you tune live on the page before you copy, so the prompt already carries your values. After that it is your AI and your words — colours, copy, sections, layout. Can I use it for client work? Yes, on any paid plan: a commercial licence, no attribution, no cap on how many sites.
