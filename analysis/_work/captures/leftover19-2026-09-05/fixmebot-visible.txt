How it works Three steps in the browser, three in Telegram The browser replaces your selection in place; Telegram answers with a message. In your browser The extension, in any text field on any site 1 Select the text you already wrote Highlight a sentence or a whole draft in any text field: Gmail, LinkedIn, a Google Doc, your CMS. 2 Pick a tool from the toolbar that appears A toolbar opens beside the selection with all 10 tools on it, or type your own instruction instead. 3 The result replaces your selection The rewritten text drops back into the field you were typing in, where the original was. Up to 15,000 characters in one selection. More about the extension In Telegram The bot, in the app you already have open 1 Send or forward a message Paste a draft, forward what someone sent you, or record a voice note. Up to 4,096 characters. 2 Tap the tool you want The bot answers with the tools as buttons: correct, translate, shorten, reply, explain. 3 Copy the answer, or keep going The result comes back as an ordinary message you can copy, forward, or run another tool on. Read all 15 answers 01 What is FixMeBot? FixMeBot is an AI writing assistant that corrects, rewrites, translates, shortens and explains text in more than 90 languages. It runs as a browser extension that works inside any text field on any site, and as a Telegram chatbot for writing on your phone. Both share one account and one character allowance. 02 Is FixMeBot free to try? Yes. Every new account gets 10,000 trial characters with no card required: 5,000 in the browser extension and 5,000 in the Telegram chatbot. Every tool except Voice to Text is unlocked during the trial, so you can test the ones you would actually pay for. 03 Do I need an account to start? You can sign in with Google in the browser extension, which takes one click and no password. The Telegram chatbot signs you in from your Telegram account automatically; linking the two later keeps one subscription across both. 04 Where does the browser extension work? In any editable text field on any website: Gmail, LinkedIn, Google Docs, your CRM, a support console, a comment box. Select the text, pick a tool from the floating toolbar, and the result replaces your selection in place. There is no separate window to copy text into and back out of. 05 How many languages does FixMeBot support? More than 90, including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Every tool works in every supported language, and translation runs between any two of them. 06 Is there a limit on how much text I can send at once? Yes: 4,096 characters per message in the Telegram chatbot and 15,000 characters per request in the browser extension. Longer documents work fine if you send them a section at a time. 07 How much does FixMeBot cost? Plans start at €4.99 per month for 200,000 characters and run to €29.99 per month for unlimited characters and the highest-accuracy models. Paying yearly costs ten months instead of twelve and raises the character allowance substantially. 08 Is my text stored or used to train models? Your text is transmitted over encrypted connections, processed to produce the result you asked for, and not retained beyond what delivering that result requires. FixMeBot does not sell your content and does not use it to train its own models. Write it once. Send it right. Join 600,000+ people who stopped re-reading their own messages.
