questions Is Raylight really free? Yes. The free plan lets you build unlimited projects with unlimited video length, and export at 1080p 60fps. Free exports carry a small Made in Raylight badge in the corner; any paid plan removes it and unlocks 4K 60fps exports, or you can remove the badge from a single export with credits. No credit card required to start. What do I get with Hobby? Clean 4K 60fps exports with no Made in Raylight badge, 300 monthly AI credits, hosted video links, video uploads up to 500MB, and a 200-slot style library. It's built for people who make videos regularly and want them to look like their own, at $15 a month or $144 a year. What does Pro add on top of Hobby? 1,000 monthly AI credits for directing edits with plain English, 5,000 MCP tool calls a week for driving Raylight from Claude or your own tools (Free includes 50 a week, Hobby 1,000, Max unlimited), embeds with share analytics for putting your videos on your own site, and 5GB video uploads. Max ($59 a month) raises that to 5,000 monthly AI credits, unlimited MCP tool calls, and 20GB uploads. Unlimited projects, live share, effects, and animations are in every plan. What's the difference between monthly and annual? Same features, different billing. Hobby is $15 a month, or $144 a year ($12 a month). Pro is $29 a month, or $288 a year ($24 a month). Max is $59 a month, or $588 a year ($49 a month). Paying annually saves about 20%, and you can switch between them anytime. Can I upload my own video footage? Yes, on every plan. Free projects can upload videos up to 50MB and 1 minute each, which covers most screen recordings and short clips. Hobby raises that to 500MB and 3 minutes per video, Pro to 5GB and 10 minutes, and Max to 20GB and 20 minutes. The length limit is separate from your finished video, which can be any length on any plan. Can I share a video without downloading it? Anyone can share a live project link with cursors and collaborators on any plan. Every paid plan adds a hosted page for the finished export, so you can send a link instead of a file, and Pro and Max can embed the video directly on your own site. Can I cancel anytime? Yes. Cancel from the billing page whenever you like and keep your plan until the end of your current billing period. There are no cancellation fees. How is Raylight different from a screen recorder? Screen recorders capture what is already on screen. Raylight is a composition tool: you arrange screenshots and clips into multi-shot sequences with 3D camera moves, depth of field, and transitions. The output looks like it cost thousands. It didn't.
