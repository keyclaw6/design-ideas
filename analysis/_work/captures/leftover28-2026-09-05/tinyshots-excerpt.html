Quick access

Always one shortcut away

TinyShots lives in your menu bar with customizable global shortcuts. Take screenshots from anywhere.

Compositions

Combine multiple images

Drag and drop multiple screenshots onto the canvas. Auto fan-layout arranges them for you. Scale, rotate, and position freely with snap guides.

Backgrounds

180+ wallpapers. Gradients. Your own.

Seven themed packs with 180+ wallpapers, plus gradient presets. Add your own backgrounds. Add blur and grain for extra texture.

Annotate

Arrows, shapes, text, emojis

Three arrow styles with curvature and thickness controls. Rectangles and ellipses with fill, stroke, and corner radius. Text in four fonts with shadow and outline. Drop emojis as stickers.

On-device AI

Remove backgrounds privately

Powered by Apple’s Vision framework. Runs entirely on your Mac, nothing leaves your machine. One click and the background is gone.

Privacy & precision

Censor, crop, and flip

Blur or pixelate sensitive regions on any layer with adjustable intensity. Crop with an interactive overlay. Flip images horizontal or vertical. All non-destructive.

Automation

CLI for agents and scripts

A full command-line interface for batch processing with preset support. Run it from scripts, or let an AI agent like Claude Code generate polished visuals for you automatically.
