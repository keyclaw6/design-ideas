## code-motion-graphics — scope

Remotion, HyperFrames / html-video, Motion Prompt, motion-anything, Claude for motion graphics, glif motion skill, CoAnimator, shot-recipe cards, Lottie/motion template libraries, After Effects → video model hand-offs, logo videos.

Exclusion: Scroll/UI motion on a web page → landing-ui-motion.

Priority `standard`. Owner aliases: Remotion, html-video, HyperFrames.
Expected primary range [10, 16]. This roster has **14** primary and **2** secondary items.
Grain rule: a primary subject keeps 6–60 analyzed items. This subject is inside that band, so it was not merged.
Seeds in `subjects.json` are hints. A seed may still be shelved; a non-seed may be primary if it answers the owner's question.

## code-motion-graphics — what the owner is trying to decide

Decide whether Remotion / html-video / Motion Prompt replace After Effects for launch films, and which shot-recipe or Lottie path is actually code-driven.

The later judge should pick a short stack, not a winner trophy. Score candidates on the axes below and keep disagreements in `claims.jsonl`.
